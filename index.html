Abstract

We present AAS2F, an ambiguity-aware speech-to-face synthesis framework that explicitly models the one-to-many nature of voice-to-face correspondence. Given a speech sample, our model generates realistic face images that are consistent with the speaker's vocal identity while capturing the diversity of plausible appearances. We combine a WavLM-based speaker embedding encoder with a LoRA-finetuned Stable Diffusion pipeline, conditioning face generation on speaker identity extracted directly from audio. Unlike prior approaches that collapse to a single face per voice, AAS2F embraces the inherent ambiguity in the voice–appearance relationship, producing multiple speaker-consistent yet visually diverse outputs.
